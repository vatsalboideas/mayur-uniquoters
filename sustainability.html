At Mayur Uniquoters, sustainability is embedded into the way we think, innovate and grow. Sustainability at Mayur

Sustainability is an integral part of our business strategy, influencing decisions across our operations, products and value chain. Guided by globally recognised frameworks, including the United Nations Global Compact (UNGC), Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), we continue to strengthen our environmental, social and governance practices while creating long-term value for all stakeholders.

Our 5P Framework

Our sustainability strategy is built around five interconnected pillars that guide how we create long-term value for our business, stakeholders and society.

  • Planet Environment Responsibility
  • Purpose Long-Term Impact
  • Product Product Excellence
  • Prosperity Economic Resilience
  • People Social Responsibility
5P Framework

  1. Purpose

    Embedding responsibility into how we govern and grow.

  2. People

    Creating a safe, inclusive workplace and stronger communities.

  3. Planet

    Reducing our environmental impact through responsible use of resources.

  4. Product

  5. Prosperity

    Creating long-term value through responsible and resilient growth.

Creating impact beyond our operations

Our responsibility extends to the communities around us. Through long-term and need-based initiatives across healthcare, education, community infrastructure, sports and the environment, we work to create meaningful impact where it matters.

Strengthening Maternal
& Child Health

For over 23 years, our immunisation programme has supported communities across 82 villages in the Phagi block. In FY 2025–26, a mobile healthcare unit operating 20–22 days each month helped provide essential vaccinations to 1,895 children and maternal vaccinations to 2,886 pregnant women, in collaboration with government health authorities.

Empowering Through
Education

We continue to strengthen access to education through scholarships and improved learning infrastructure. In FY 2025–26, 10 new classrooms were constructed across three government schools, expected to benefit approximately 1,100 students, while 280 new furniture sets were provided across multiple schools. Through the long-running SK Poddar Scholarship initiative, meritorious students from government schools continue to receive financial support towards their education.

Creating Spaces for
Women to Learn & Grow

A new community hall and library in Dhodhsar has been developed to promote women’s education and empowerment. Designed to accommodate up to 100 girls at a time, the facility is expected to benefit nearly 3,000 women and girls from Dhodhsar and neighbouring Singod Kalan.
