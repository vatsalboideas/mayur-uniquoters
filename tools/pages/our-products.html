01

PVC Vinyl

Where It All Began

Our founding material and still our highest-volume line, PVC Vinyl combines dimensional stability, water resistance and colour retention for diverse applications.

With 7 Release Paper Transfer Coating lines, Mayur manufactures PVC Vinyl for applications across automotive, marine and heavy-duty furnishing.

02

PU

A Softer, More Supple Alternative

Designed to offer a feel closer to natural leather, Mayur’s PU material combines softness and flexibility for applications including premium automotive interiors, fashion accessories and footwear uppers.

Our dedicated PU manufacturing facility at Morena, Madhya Pradesh has an initial installed capacity of 5 million linear metres per year, expandable up to 20 million linear metres.

03

PU-PVC Hybrid

Combining a PU skin with a PVC base, PU-PVC Hybrid brings together the look and feel of PU with the rigidity and dimensional stability of PVC.

Its dual-layer construction makes it suitable for applications where hardware attachment or structural shape retention is important.

04

Sustainable Materials

UNICO

Developed using bio-PUR and bio-based yarns, UNICO incorporates more sustainable inputs into material development.

05

Sustainable Materials

PAVO

Developed using recycled yarns and bio-PVC, PAVO brings together recycled and bio-based inputs.
