About Us

Since 1994, Mayur Uniquoters has evolved into one of India's largest manufacturers of synthetic leather and among the top global players in the segment.

Driven by innovation, advanced manufacturing and responsible practices, we develop high-performance material solutions for the automotive, footwear, furnishing and lifestyle industries, delivering quality that is trusted across global markets.

History

From a single manufacturing facility in 1994 to a globally trusted material solutions company, our journey has been defined by innovation, expansion and a relentless pursuit of excellence. Every milestone reflects our commitment to creating better products, building stronger partnerships and growing responsibly.
