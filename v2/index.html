Our Story

About Us

Since 1994, Mayur Uniquoters has evolved into one of India's largest manufacturers of synthetic leather and among the top global players in the segment.

Driven by innovation, advanced manufacturing and responsible practices, we develop high-performance material solutions for the automotive, footwear, furnishing and lifestyle industries, delivering quality that is trusted across global markets.

Since 1994

History

From a single manufacturing facility in 1994 to a globally trusted material solutions company, our journey has been defined by innovation, expansion and a relentless pursuit of excellence.

  1. 1994

    Mayur Uniquoters was founded with its first manufacturing facility in Jaitpura, Jaipur.

  2. 1995

    First export shipment, marking the beginning of our global journey.

  3. 2012

    Established our wholly owned subsidiary in Texas, USA.

  4. 2015‑2018

    Expanded manufacturing capabilities, strengthened quality systems and introduced new production technologies.

  5. 2019

    Became a member of the United Nations Global Compact, reinforcing our commitment to responsible growth.

  6. 2020‑2024

    Published our first Sustainability Report, expanded global operations and strengthened manufacturing infrastructure with new facilities and subsidiaries.
